# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

RuleLoom contributors, maintainers, and participants commit to making project
spaces welcoming and safe, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and
orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that support an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, experiences, and technical backgrounds;
- giving and accepting constructive, evidence-based feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and repairing harm;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users;
- treating negative experimental results as useful evidence rather than failure
  by the person reporting them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, or attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- pressuring contributors to reveal proprietary data, private prompts, or
  personal information;
- manipulation or selective reporting of project evidence to target a person;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public or private spaces. Project spaces include code
review, issues, discussions, community chat, events, and direct communication
about project work.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the moderation or private-contact
mechanism advertised by the project's current distribution page. Do not include
sensitive incident details in a public issue. If no private mechanism is
advertised, ask for a private reporting route without disclosing the incident
and withhold details until one is provided.

Maintainers who receive a report must protect the reporter's privacy, avoid
retaliation, disclose conflicts of interest, and recuse themselves when
necessary. Reports will be evaluated in context. Responses may include a private
correction, warning, temporary restriction, removal from project spaces, or a
permanent ban, depending on impact and pattern.

Knowingly false reports intended to harass another participant are themselves a
violation. An unsubstantiated good-faith report is not.
